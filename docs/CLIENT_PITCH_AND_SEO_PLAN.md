## PART 2 — SEO Editorial Plan: Durable Content vs Just Trending News

### The strategic distinction most publishers miss

**Trending wires** (scores, injury updates, trade rumors) are **fast, perishable traffic**: high volume from Google Discover, Facebook, and search for 24–48 hours, then decay to near-zero. They are your *top-of-funnel reach engine*.

**Durable editorial content** (reviews, comparisons, explainers, schedules, standings) are **slow, compounding traffic**: they rank on Google for 6–24 months, capture high-intent searches ("best betting sites philippines", "pba schedule 2026"), and convert directly into sportsbook signups and newsletter subscribers. They are your *revenue engine*.

**The winning model combines both (60/30/10 split):**

| % of editorial effort | Content type | Role | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **60%** | Wire & trending news (~50/day, AI-assisted) | Discover reach, FB shares, same-day authority | Game previews, recaps, injury wires |
| **30%** | Durable pillars (1–3/week, editor-written) | Google rankings, citations, money pages | Sportsbook reviews, how-to-watch guides, odds explainers |
| **10%** | Narrative features (weekly) | Brand affinity, AI citations, email digest | Kai Sotto deep-dive, PBA draft previews, PVL dynasty story |

### Five durable pillars to build (with money-page keywords)

**Pillar 1 — Sports Betting Philippines (money pillar)**
- *Money pages:* "Best sports betting sites Philippines 2026", "Is sports betting legal in the Philippines" (PAGCOR guide), "OKBet review & how to sign up with GCash", "ArenaPlus review", "WinZir review", "How to read decimal odds — Philippines guide"
- *Intent:* commercial; every reader is a candidate for sportsbook affiliate links

**Pillar 2 — NBA Philippines (reach + intent pillar)**
- *Money pages:* "NBA standings 2026 Philippines time", "How to watch the NBA in the Philippines (legally)", "NBA schedule today (PHT)", "NBA team & player power rankings — weekly"
- *Spokes:* daily AI predictions slate, game previews, fantasy tips, bettor's guide per matchup

**Pillar 3 — PBA & Philippine Basketball (local authority)**
- *Money pages:* "PBA schedule & standings 2026", "PBA Governors' Cup bracket & how to watch", "PBA draft 2026 prospects", "Gilas Pilipinas schedule & roster"
- *Spokes:* game recaps, series trackers, Kai Sotto tracker, collegiate (UAAP/NCAA)

**Pillar 4 — Global Sports for US/PH audiences (US-demand pillar)**
- *Pages:* "NFL schedule & standings 2026", "Premier League & UCL guides", "Soccer top-teams preview", "How to bet on NFL from the Philippines (legal options)"

**Pillar 5 — Picks, Odds & Predictions (the conversion machine)**
- *Pages:* Daily AI predictions slate (permanent URL updated daily with datePublished), weekly best-bets, biggest-underdog trackers, odds-movement explainers

### Article architecture (per our [ai-extractable playbook](https://github.com/LA-claude-26/skills-sync/tree/main/team/leadadvisors-core))
Every substantive article (1,500–2,500 words) follows this formula:
1. **Title** — promises the exact answer, includes the year
2. **First 100 words** — direct answer + Key Takeaways box
3. **Question-headed H2 sections** answered immediately in sentence one
4. **Sourced numbers** — every stat linked to primary source (PAGCOR, NBA.com, PBA.ph) with year
5. **Comparison table** — minimum 5–8 features with a clear top pick
6. **FAQ** with `FAQPage` schema
7. **"Where to go next" decision box** → nearest money page or newsletter CTA
8. **Inline related modules** (after sections 3 & 6) + continue-reading carousel (4 cards)
9. **Schema layer:** Article, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Organization, `datePublished` + `dateModified`

### AI answer engine visibility (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot)
- **robots.txt** explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended
- **llms.txt** markdown manifest at root describing the site's coverage and key pages
- Question-answer H2s + sourced numbers + clear authorship are exactly what answer engines quote as named sources
- Cloudflare bot protection configured to exempt verified AI crawlers

## PART 4 — Very Simple Remarketing Plan

> **The insight:** 70–80% of wire traffic from Google Discover/FB will be one-time visitors. Without capture, each visitor has near-zero lifetime value. With capture, they become daily readers, newsletter sponsors' eyeballs, and sportsbook signups.

### Flow: Capture → Nurture → Convert

```
One-time wire visitor (Discover / FB / Google)
        ↓
Mid-article CTA box + sticky mobile footer bar
        ↓
SPORTY DAILY email (7:00 AM PHT) ──→ 3-day welcome sequence ──→ sponsored sends + affiliate picks
        ↓
Meta pixel & GA4 retargeting lists (readers who clicked NBA/PBA/odds content)
        ↓
Remarketing ads: "today's AI picks are in" → return visits → conversions
        ↓
Viber / Telegram community (Philippines-native) for breaking-wire alerts
```

### Four concrete remarketing assets to build

**1. SPORTY DAILY — the anchor asset**
- Free 3-minute morning email at **7:00 AM PHT**: yesterday's results + today's must-know lines + AI pick of the day + Philippine angle
- Capture points: hero band on homepage, mid-article box (after paragraph 6), exit-intent modal, sticky mobile bar, dedicated `/newsletter/` page
- Target: **≥1.0% capture rate** of total sessions; at 500k monthly sessions = **~5,000 new subscribers/mo**

**2. Three-day email welcome sequence**
- *Email 1 (instant):* "Welcome — here's today's best bet + how our odds work (decimal)"
- *Email 2 (day 2):* "The 3 tools our editors use to find edges" + link to the odds board
- *Email 3 (day 3):* "Pick your leagues" (segmentation: NBA / PBA / NFL / Soccer) + first sponsored partner shoutout (OKBet)

**3. Pixel-based retargeting (simple, cheap)**
- Install **Meta Pixel** + **GA4**; create audiences: `viewed /betting/*` (commercial intent), `viewed /nba/predictions` (bettors), `visited 2+ times` (fans)
- Run a $10–$20/day remarketing campaign on Facebook (where PH sports fans live): *"Today's AI picks are in — Boston −6.5 is the model's best play"* → return visits at ~$0.08–0.25 per click
- Once email list hits 10k, create lookalike audiences for new subscriber acquisition

**4. Viber & Telegram community (Philippines-first)**
- 90% of Filipinos with internet use messaging apps; a **"Sporty Wires" Viber channel** pushing 3–5 breaking alerts/day converts 3–5x better than email for younger fans
- Every alert links back to the full article (traffic + capture) and every article ends with "get wires faster on Viber"

### Remarketing KPIs to track weekly

| KPI | Target (month 3) |
|---|---|
| Newsletter subscribers | 5,000+ |
| Capture rate (% of sessions) | ≥ 1.0% |
| Email open / CTR | ≥ 35% / ≥ 4% |
| Returning-visitor share | ≥ 25% |
| Sportsbook affiliate clicks / month | 2,500+ |
| Viber community members | 2,000+ |
